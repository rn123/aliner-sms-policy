Privacy Policy — A-Liner SMS Status Bridge

Last updated: 2026-05-14
Operator: Ray Nowell (Sole Proprietor) — ray.nowell@gmail.com

What this service is

A personal SMS-based query interface that lets the operator check the status of their own off-grid RV monitoring system (battery state of charge, solar production, Starlink connectivity, propane level, weather, location) from their own mobile phone. The recipient and the operator are the same individual.

How consent is given

This is a Sole Proprietor program with a single recipient who is also the sole sender. There is no public opt-in form or third-party enrollment. Consent is given by the account holder entering their own mobile number into a configuration file on the RV monitoring system they own and operate; the system enforces a single-number allowlist so no other number can be enrolled. Consent is withdrawn by replying STOP or removing the number from the configuration; replying START restores service.

Data collected

  • Inbound SMS message body (a single keyword such as soc or solar).
  • Inbound SMS sender phone number, used only to verify the sender is the registered operator.
  • Twilio routing metadata (timestamp, message SID) handled by Twilio per their standard messaging API.

How data is used

Inbound SMS bodies are matched against a fixed keyword list and dispatched to a corresponding status reply. The sender phone number is checked against an allowlist of one (the registered operator's mobile number) and discarded after each request.

Data retention

Twilio retains message logs per its own retention policy (see twilio.com/legal/privacy). The receiving Home Assistant system does not log inbound SMS bodies beyond the immediate dispatch handler.

Third-party sharing

None. SMS data is not shared with third parties, sold, rented, or used for marketing. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Marketing

No marketing or promotional messages are sent. The system only replies to inbound queries from the registered operator's verified mobile number.
