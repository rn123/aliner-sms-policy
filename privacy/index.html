Privacy Policy — A-Liner SMS Status Bridge

Last updated: 2026-05-19
Operator: Ray Nowell (Sole Proprietor) — ray.nowell@gmail.com

What this service is

An automated SMS application that replies with the status of an off-grid RV monitoring system (battery state of charge, solar production, Starlink connectivity, propane level, weather, location) when an authorized recipient texts a keyword to the service number. The application originates every outbound message; recipients are the system owner and authorized household members.

How consent is given

This is a Sole Proprietor program. There is no public opt-in form, advertisement, or third-party enrollment. Each recipient consents by having their mobile number registered with the application by the owner; enrollment is limited to the owner and authorized household members, and the application enforces an allowlist so that only registered numbers receive replies. Consent is withdrawn by replying STOP or removing the number from the configuration; replying START restores service.

Data collected

  • Inbound SMS message body (a single keyword such as soc or solar).
  • Inbound SMS sender phone number, used only to verify the sender is an authorized recipient.
  • Twilio routing metadata (timestamp, message SID) handled by Twilio per their standard messaging API.

How data is used

Inbound SMS bodies are matched against a fixed keyword list and dispatched to a corresponding status reply. The sender phone number is checked against the allowlist of authorized numbers and discarded after each request.

Data retention

Twilio retains message logs per its own retention policy (see twilio.com/legal/privacy). The receiving Home Assistant system does not log inbound SMS bodies beyond the immediate dispatch handler.

Third-party sharing

None. SMS data is not shared with third parties, sold, rented, or used for marketing. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Marketing

No marketing or promotional messages are sent. The system only replies to inbound queries from authorized, verified mobile numbers.
